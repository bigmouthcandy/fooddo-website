foodDo

Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026

foodDo is a meal planner and macro tracker made by Phillip Cooke in Northern Ireland. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes. In short: your food diary is yours, it lives in your own iCloud, and foodDo has no accounts, adverts or tracking.

Your food diary

The foods, meals, amounts and targets you enter are stored on your iPhone and synced to your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they appear on your other devices. This data is held by Apple under your Apple Account. I can't see it, and it is never sent to me or anyone else.

Community foods (optional)

When you create a food that has a barcode, you can choose to share it so other foodDo users can find it by scanning the same barcode. This is on by default and can be switched off on each food before saving. A shared food includes only:

  • the barcode, product name and brand
  • the nutrition values and serving size from the label
  • the version of foodDo used

It doesn't include your name, email, Apple Account or anything from your food diary. Shared foods are stored in foodDo's public CloudKit database. Apple attaches an anonymous, app-specific ID to each entry, which I can't link to who you are. Names are checked against a word filter, and I may edit or remove entries to keep the list accurate.

Reporting a problem with a food

If you use "Report a problem", foodDo sends the food's name, brand, barcode, nutrition values, the problem you picked, any details you type, and the app version. Like community foods, reports don't include your name or contact details.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode that isn't already in foodDo, the barcode number is looked up in the foodDo community list and then sent to Open Food Facts, a free public food database, to find the product. Only the barcode number is sent. Open Food Facts has its own privacy policy.

Camera and label scanning

foodDo uses the camera to scan barcodes and to read nutrition labels. Photos are read on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device text recognition and, on supported iPhones, Apple Intelligence. They are not saved by foodDo and are never uploaded.

Sharing a food or meal

When you share a food or meal, its name and nutrition are packed into the link itself. Opening the link on fooddo.app shows the food using code that runs in your browser. The food details are in the part of the link after the "#", which browsers don't send to the website, so the details aren't sent to or stored on any server.

What foodDo doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No adverts
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No selling or sharing of your data

If you install a test version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with me, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.

Deleting your data

Deleting a food or day in foodDo removes it from your iCloud too. You can remove all foodDo data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → foodDo. To have a community food you shared removed, email me with its barcode.

Children

foodDo is not aimed at children under 13.
